Recordatorios ("reminders" in Spanish) is a calendar with tasks, appointments, reminders and private notes that works entirely on your phone: no account, no server and no internet permission. Your data never leaves the device. The app is in Spanish.

<b>Calendar</b>
• Tasks that keep reminding you until you mark them as "Done".
• Appointments with advance reminders (on time, 1 hour before, the day before…).
• Repeats: every day, week, month or year, and skipped days (3 on, 2 off).
• Colours, notes and birthdays with the age. Important items, written in CAPITALS, remind you earlier.
• Search.

<b>Reminders</b>
Exact alarms on the phone itself, with a "Done" button in the notification. They are rescheduled automatically when the phone restarts.

<b>Locked notes</b>
Text notes with colours and pinning. Locked notes are encrypted on the phone with your password (PBKDF2 + NaCl secretbox); if you forget it they cannot be recovered.

<b>Widgets</b>
Upcoming (with "Done"), Month with coloured dots, and Add / New note buttons.

<b>Backup</b>
Export everything to a file and restore it on another phone (locked notes stay encrypted).

Light and dark theme. Free software (GPL-3.0).
